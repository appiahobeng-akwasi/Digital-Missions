Legal

Privacy.

This site collects as little as possible.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies for tracking. It does not include third-party tracking pixels, advertising tags, marketing automation scripts, or chat widgets. We use a privacy-first analytics service to record aggregate, non-identifying page views. No personal data is sold or shared.

What we do with email you send us

If you write to hello@digitalmissions.technology, your message is read by the Digital Missions team and used only to respond to your inquiry and continue any conversation it begins. We do not add you to a mailing list. We do not share your message with third parties unless required by law.

Data hosted by third parties

Our website is hosted by an infrastructure provider that retains standard server logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and operational purposes. These logs are not used to build user profiles. Email is delivered through a standard mail provider.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change. The version above is current as of 2026.
